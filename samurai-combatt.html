About Katana Kid

★ Meet Luqman, Chief Game Designer ★

Hi, adventurer! The game you are playing began as an idea in Luqman's imagination.

Brave little samurai, sneaky ninjas, hopping bandit frogs, spooky paper lanterns and a giant Red Oni guarding a magic shard: this whole world of Katana Kid grew out of Luqman's creativity and vision.

That is what game designers do: they imagine something new, then work to make it real. Thank you, Luqman, for dreaming it up!

Remember: every hero starts as a beginner. Keep practising, keep imagining, and one day you might build your own game too!
